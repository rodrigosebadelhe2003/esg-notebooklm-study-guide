## About the Project

This repository was developed as part of the **DIO NotebookLM Challenge**.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how **Artificial Intelligence** can support active learning through **Google NotebookLM**, combining official technical documentation, multimedia resources and prompt engineering to build a structured study guide on **Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG)**.

Instead of using AI only to generate summaries, this project explores critical analysis, document comparison and knowledge organization based on internationally recognized references.

---

# Objectives

- Build a thematic notebook using Google NotebookLM.
- Study ESG concepts from reliable international references.
- Compare different ESG frameworks.
- Practice Prompt Engineering techniques.
- Organize knowledge into reusable study material.
- Demonstrate AI-assisted learning workflows.

# How NotebookLM Was Used

The documents and videos were uploaded into NotebookLM to create a thematic knowledge base.

NotebookLM was used to:

- Compare information across different sources.
- Identify recurring ESG concepts.
- Generate structured summaries.
- Produce comparison tables.
- Build a glossary.
- Support active learning through iterative prompting.

---

# Prompt Engineering

Rather than requesting generic summaries, progressively more analytical prompts were developed.

## Prompt 1

> Explain ESG based on all uploaded sources, identifying the Environmental, Social and Governance pillars.

---

## Prompt 2

> Compare the approaches adopted by the IFC ESG Guidebook, IFC Performance Standards and the World Bank Environmental and Social Framework.

---

## Prompt 3

> Considering a large infrastructure project, explain how ESG principles should be incorporated during planning, licensing and operation.

---

## Prompt 4

> Assume the role of an Environmental Consultant. Explain how an Environmental Engineer could apply the concepts presented in the uploaded documents.

---

## Prompt 5

> Identify similarities, differences and possible limitations among the three official documents.

---

## Prompt 6

> Produce a structured study guide including summaries, glossary, comparison tables and ESG indicators.

---

# Prompt Iteration ("Scars")

## Initial Situation

The first prompts produced generic answers with little comparison between the documents.

## Improvements

The prompts were refined by:

- requesting comparisons between sources;
- defining professional scenarios;
- assigning expert roles to the AI;
- requesting structured outputs;
- requesting synthesis instead of isolated summaries.

These refinements significantly improved the quality and depth of the responses.

## Key Takeaways

- ESG combines environmental, social and governance dimensions into decision-making.
- IFC Performance Standards provide practical guidance for managing environmental and social risks.
- The World Bank Environmental and Social Framework expands ESG implementation through ten Environmental and Social Standards.
- Effective stakeholder engagement is fundamental throughout project life cycles.
- Environmental risk management should begin during project planning.

# Glossary

| Concept | Definition |
|----------|------------|
| ESG | Environmental, Social and Governance framework used to evaluate sustainability performance. |
| Stakeholder | Individuals or organizations affected by or capable of influencing a project. |
| Due Diligence | Process used to identify environmental and social risks before decision-making. |
| Materiality | Identification of the most relevant sustainability topics for an organization. |
| Environmental Risk | Potential negative environmental impacts associated with a project. |
| Social Risk | Potential impacts affecting workers, communities and society. |
| Governance | Structures and processes used to direct and manage organizations. |

# Lessons Learned

This project demonstrated that AI becomes significantly more valuable when combined with:

- high-quality sources;
- well-designed prompts;
- critical thinking;
- iterative refinement;
- structured documentation.

Rather than replacing the learning process, NotebookLM acted as an intelligent study assistant, helping organize information, compare technical references and consolidate knowledge.
